GlobeLayers — live 3D weather globe

GlobeLayers is a live 3D weather globe. Spin it, zoom in, and read today's weather anywhere on Earth, straight from open forecast data.

Layers

  • Temperature — air temperature 2 m above ground, in °C.
  • Wind — animated streamlines of the 10 m wind; speed and direction on hover.
  • Clouds — total cloud cover, in %.
  • Rain — precipitation rate, in mm/h.
  • Pressure — mean sea-level pressure with isobars, in hPa.
  • Humidity — relative humidity 2 m above ground, in %.
  • PM2.5 and Dust — fine particles and desert dust near the surface, in µg/m³.
  • Labels and Rivers — cities with the value of the active layer, countries, and major rivers.

Data & freshness

Weather layers come from the NOAA GFS model at 0.25° resolution and are refreshed every hour; air quality comes from NOAA GEFS-Aerosols, refreshed every 3 hours. The globe shows the forecast valid for the current hour, taken from the latest available model run; the banner always shows the run and valid time.
