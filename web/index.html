    GlobeLayers — live 3D weather globe

    GlobeLayers is a live 3D weather globe. Spin it, zoom in, and read today's weather anywhere on Earth, straight from open forecast data.

    Layers

    • Temperature — air temperature 2 m above ground, in °C.
    • Wind — animated streamlines of the 10 m wind; speed and direction on hover.
    • Clouds — total cloud cover, in %.
    • Rain — precipitation rate, in mm/h.
    • Pressure — mean sea-level pressure with isobars, in hPa.
    • Humidity — relative humidity 2 m above ground, in %.
    • PM2.5 and Dust — fine particles and desert dust near the surface, in µg/m³.
    • Labels and Rivers — cities with the value of the active layer, countries, and major rivers.
    • Search and My location — find any town of more than 1,000 inhabitants, or fly to where you are.
    • Timeline — the forecast for the next 48 hours: drag the slider or press play.

    Data & freshness

    Weather layers come from the NOAA GFS model at 0.25° resolution; air quality comes from NOAA GEFS-Aerosols. Four times a day, each new model run brings a 48-hour forecast in 3-hour steps, and the globe blends smoothly between steps. The banner shows the model run; the timeline shows the time you are looking at.

    Privacy

    GlobeLayers uses cookie-free audience measurement by Cloudflare Web Analytics. No personal data is collected and there are no advertising trackers. The “My location” button uses your device's location only in your browser: it is never sent or stored.

    GlobeLayers, a live 3D weather globe

    GlobeLayers draws the weather of the whole planet on one rotating globe. Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and open the layer menu to switch between temperature, wind, clouds, rain, pressure, humidity, PM2.5 and dust.

    To find a place, use the magnifier: it searches every town above 1,000 inhabitants. The pin button centres the globe on where you are. Your position is read once by your browser and never leaves it, and it is never written into a link or a saved image.

    The slider at the bottom steps through the forecast, three hours at a time, over two days. Press play to watch the weather move. The camera button saves what you see as an image, or shares it, and the address bar keeps your view so a link opens the same place. Three switches next to the layer menu turn on the animated wind, place names and rivers.

    Weather fields come from the NOAA GFS model at 0.25 degree resolution, and the air quality layers from NOAA GEFS-Aerosols. The data refreshes four times a day. Each map page of this site opens the globe on one layer, and the menu in the top left lists them all.
